The big picture

How many visitors come, and what happened when the border closed

In 1991, visitors made 2.4 million trips to Australia. By 2019, that had almost quadrupled to 9.5 million. Then COVID-19 hit, and in 2021 just 246,240 visitors arrived all year.

Tourists returned once the border reopened in February 2022. But in 2025, arrivals were still below their 2019 peak.
